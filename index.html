FAQ

What does the FAQ generator create?

Ten FAQ rows for an AI customer service bot, each with five fields: Category, Question, Standard answer, Needs human, and Trigger keywords. You can preview them, copy them as text, or download a CSV.

Does it make up prices or details?

No. Answers only use what you type in. Prices appear only for services where you entered a price; if a field is empty, the answer says the information isn't available yet and the row is set to Needs human = Yes.

Which questions always go to a human?

Refunds, complaints, allergies or reactions, and guaranteed results are always set to Needs human = Yes, and their answers never promise an outcome.

Can I open the CSV in Excel?

Yes. The CSV is saved as UTF-8 with a BOM so Excel and Google Sheets open it with the correct characters.

Is my shop data uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs in your browser and the page works offline. Nothing you type is sent to a server.

Is this medical or legal advice?

No. It generates customer-service templates from your input. Allergy, refund and complaint cases should be handled by your staff; anyone with a serious reaction should seek medical care.
